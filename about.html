Our Story

Bridging the Critical Care Divide in Eastern Uttar Pradesh

Established in 1999, AIS Hospital was founded on a singular conviction: that no family in our region should ever have to travel hundreds of kilometers to metropolitan capitals for emergency cardiac care, high-risk neurosurgery, or advanced neonatal life support.

What started as a modest 30-bed community clinic has blossomed into a comprehensive 350-bed tertiary institution equipped with 8 modular operation theatres, a flat-panel digital Cardiac Cath Lab, Level-III NICU, and a 24×7 polytrauma emergency department.

"Our measure of success has never been the number of beds we fill, but the number of smiles and healthy heartbeats we send back home to their families."

— Medical Director, AIS Hospital
